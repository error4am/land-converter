Quick Explanation

What 10 Marla Means

A 10 Marla plot is a common mid-size residential option in Pakistan. It often appears in housing society listings where people want more space than a 5 Marla plot but still compare the area in square feet for construction planning.

Since 1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft, multiplying by 10 gives 2722.5 sq ft. This also means 10 Marla equals 0.5 Kanal.
