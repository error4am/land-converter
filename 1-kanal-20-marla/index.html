Measurement Reference

Pakistan Land Measurement: 1 Kanal = 20 Marla

In Pakistani property listings, 1 Kanal = 20 Marla. This relation is the simplest way to compare larger plots with Marla-based pricing and residential plot sizes.

Core Formula

1 Kanal Conversion Values

  • 1 Kanal = 20 Marla
  • 1 Kanal = 5,445 square feet
  • 1 Kanal = about 505.86 square meters
  • 1 Kanal = 0.125 acre

Why This Measurement Matters

Buyers may see the same property described in Kanal, Marla, square feet or square meters. Knowing that 1 Kanal equals 20 Marla helps you compare listings, market rates and payment schedules without mixing units.
