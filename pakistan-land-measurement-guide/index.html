Reference Guide

Pakistan Land Measurement Guide for Marla, Kanal and Acre

This guide explains the main land units used in Pakistan - Marla, Kanal, Acre, square feet and square meters - so you can compare listings, plans and official documents without confusion. Use it as the reference hub behind all of the converter pages.

Marla

Residential Base Unit

Marla is one of the most common plot-size units in Pakistan. On this site, we use the standard conversion of 1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft = 25.29 sq m.

Kanal

Larger Plot Unit

Kanal is used for bigger residential or agricultural plots. The core relation is 1 Kanal = 20 Marla = 5445 sq ft = 505.86 sq m.

Acre

Large Land Unit

Acre is common for farms, larger land holdings and commercial use. The exact relation is 1 Acre = 43,560 sq ft = 4046.86 sq m.

Square Feet

Pricing and Covered Area

Square feet are often used to compare construction cost, floor plans and price per square foot. Buyers use it to translate Marla and Kanal listings into a more detailed area figure.

Square Meters

Metric Comparison

Square meters are useful for surveys, maps, engineering drawings and official records. They become especially important when a listing is local but the supporting document is metric.

Why Units Get Mixed

Real-World Confusion

In Pakistan, one property ad may use Marla, another may use Kanal, while the map or file uses square meters. This is why quick conversion tools matter so much during buying, selling and planning.
