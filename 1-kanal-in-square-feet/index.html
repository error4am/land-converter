Quick Explanation

What 1 Kanal Means

A 1 Kanal plot is a common larger residential size in Pakistan. It often appears in premium housing society listings, farmhouses and plots where buyers want to compare bigger land area against construction cost or market value.

Since 1 Kanal = 20 Marla and 1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft, the full plot size becomes 5445 sq ft.
