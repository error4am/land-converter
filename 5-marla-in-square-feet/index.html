Quick Explanation

What 5 Marla Means

A 5 Marla plot is a very common residential size in Pakistan. Buyers often search this exact query because developers, agents and housing societies describe plots in Marla, while floor plans and covered area comparisons usually happen in square feet.

Since 1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft, multiplying by 5 gives 1361.25 sq ft.
