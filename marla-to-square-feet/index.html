Convert Marla to Sq Ft

Formula: Marla x 272.25 = square feet.

Common Marla Plot Sizes

1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft. 5 Marla = 1361.25 sq ft. 10 Marla = 2722.5 sq ft. These are some of the most common property searches in Pakistan because listings are often described in Marla first and square feet second.

If you are checking a home listing, a plot advertisement or a construction estimate, converting Marla to square feet makes it easier to compare floor plans, covered area and price per square foot.
