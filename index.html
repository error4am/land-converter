Land Units

Know the units before you compare the prices.

The biggest confusion in property searches usually comes from mixed units. One listing says Marla, another says Kanal, and a map uses square meters. This section keeps the core formulas in one place.

Marla

1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft = 25.2929 sq m.

Often used for residential plots and house listings.

Kanal

1 Kanal = 20 Marla = 5445 sq ft = 505.857 sq m.

Common for larger residential and agricultural plots.

Acre

1 Acre = 43,560 sq ft = 4046.86 sq m.

Useful for farms, commercial land and bigger holdings.

Square Feet

Best for covered area, house sizes and rate comparisons.

Square Meter
